Why Poker Lineup

You know you saw something on this player… but you can't find it.

A serious live player picks up valuable reads on opponents, session after session. The value of those reads stays the same over time. Memory does not.

Capture at the table

Build an opponent's profile in a few taps while you play: who they are, how they play, what you just saw. Nothing to fill in later.

Find it months later

Search your profiles by name or by traits. Every note is dated, so you see what you saw, and when.

Stay in the game

Built to be fast: few taps, few things on screen, so your attention stays on the table.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What does Poker Lineup do?

It lets you create opponent profiles, record dated reads on how they play, and find them again later, on your phone and even without a connection.

Is it a GTO solver?

No. Poker Lineup does not compute theoretical strategy. It keeps what you actually observed about specific players.

Is the app available?

Poker Lineup is in development and heading to the App Store and Google Play. This page will be updated at launch.

How do I sign in?

With Google, with Apple, or with an email address and a password. The app only receives your name and email address from Google or Apple, to create your account.

What happens to my data?

Your notes belong to you. We use no advertising and no tracking. You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app. Details are in the privacy policy.
